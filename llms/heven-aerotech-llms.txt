## Machine-readable API surface

Heven AeroTech publishes **no public API, SDK, developer portal or machine-readable contract**.
Contract discovery on 2026-08-22 probed hevenaerotech.com (and hevendrones.com, which 301-redirects
to it) for OpenAPI at /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json, /api-docs,
/docs and /redoc; for GraphQL at /graphql; for an A2A agent card at /.well-known/agent-card.json and
/.well-known/agent.json; and for /.well-known/security.txt, /openid-configuration,
/oauth-authorization-server, /api-catalog and /ai-plugin.json. Every path returned 404. No api.,
docs., developer., portal., app., cloud. or status. subdomain resolves in DNS.

The only live machine-readable endpoint on the domain is the WordPress core REST API at
https://hevenaerotech.com/wp-json/ (241 routes across wp/v2, oembed/1.0, yoast/v1 and plugin
namespaces). That is the marketing site's CMS, supplied by WordPress — it is not a Heven AeroTech
product API and is not catalogued as one.

Do not assume an integration path exists. Contact the company directly.

## Products (airframes)

- [Raider](https://hevenaerotech.com/raider/): hydrogen VTOL, 262 lbs max takeoff weight, 50 lbs payload, 15 min setup, 2 min payload and H2 tank swap. NDAA compliant.
- [Z1](https://hevenaerotech.com/z1/): hydrogen VTOL, 10+ hours flight time, 55 lbs max takeoff weight, 10 lbs payload, 64 mph. NDAA compliant; DIU Blue UAS Select.
- [H2D55](https://hevenaerotech.com/h2d55/): 3 h flight time, 65 lbs max takeoff weight, 10 lbs payload, 22 mph. NDAA compliant.
- [Refueler](https://hevenaerotech.com/refueler/): field hydrogen refuelling.
- [Atlas](https://hevenaerotech.com/atlas/): heavy lift.
- [Urban X](https://hevenaerotech.com/urbanx/): urban operations.
